# Tender Category Library

## Purpose
This library classifies tender categories for LMCP AutoQuote planning and profitability screening without changing workflow behavior.

## Governance Boundaries
- Classification is advisory.
- Classification does not auto-qualify or auto-block live tenders.
- Final decisions remain manual and governed.

## Already Blocked
These categories are already excluded for LMCP AutoQuote:
- medical consumables
- IT equipment
- petrol
- diesel
- catering

## Allowed Categories
These categories are generally aligned with supply-and-delivery tenders:
- office furniture
- PPE
- electrical consumables
- construction materials
- cleaning chemicals
- stationery
- hardware and tools
- packaging
- janitorial supplies
- safety signage
- plumbing consumables

## Excellent Targets
High-fit categories for future quotation work:
- office furniture
- PPE
- stationery
- cleaning chemicals
- packaging
- janitorial supplies
- hardware and tools

## Good Targets
Reasonable categories when supplier evidence is strong:
- electrical consumables
- construction materials
- safety signage
- plumbing consumables
- light industrial consumables
- general office supplies

## Avoid Targets
Categories that should usually be screened out or escalated:
- medical consumables
- IT equipment
- petrol
- diesel
- catering
- highly specialized equipment
- functionality-heavy service tenders
- bids with unclear delivery scope
- bids requiring unusual certification that is not already documented

## Screening Notes
- Favor supply-and-delivery work.
- Favor repeatable procurement patterns.
- Favor categories with stable supplier coverage.
- Avoid categories with unpredictable compliance overhead.
- Avoid categories that depend on specialized accreditation unless governance explicitly approves the exception.

## Classification Fields
When a category is reviewed, record:
- category name
- target tier
- margin expectation
- supplier coverage confidence
- province coverage confidence
- complexity rating
- evidence notes
- final review date

# Supervised-Live Rollout Profile

The initial supervised-live rollout starts small and deliberately stays below full capacity.

## Operator Ramp

- Day 1-2: 3 operators, 25-50 RFQs/day
- Day 3-4: 5 operators, 50-100 RFQs/day
- Day 5-7: 5-7 operators, 100-200 RFQs/day

## Operational Focus

- Queue stability
- Governance discipline
- Telemetry usefulness
- Evidence handling
- Source quality
- Operator fatigue

## Source Scope

- Tier 1: active, small stable starter set
- Tier 2: active, conservative subset
- Tier 3: off initially
- Tier 4: passive only

## Control Policy

- No autonomous procurement execution
- No auto-submission
- No auto-approval
- No bypass of `review_ready`
- No bypass of proof capture
- No scaling until stability is demonstrated

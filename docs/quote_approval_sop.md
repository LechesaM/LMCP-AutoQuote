# Quote Approval SOP

## Approval Prerequisites

- Quote pack is complete
- Pricing thresholds are satisfied
- No exclusion rule applies
- Required source documents are available
- The operator can explain the approval decision

## Pricing Checks

- Minimum profit is at least R30,000
- Minimum supply margin is at least 25%
- No hidden autonomous adjustment is allowed

## Artifact Checks

- Quote PDF is present
- Quote JSON is present
- Buyer pricing schedule is present where required
- Quote pack contents are internally consistent

## Buyer Pricing Schedule Checks

- Check the completed buyer schedule is present and aligned with the source RFQ
- Check there are no unresolved mismatches

## Manual Approval Recording

- Record approval only through the approved workflow path
- Keep the approval record append-only
- Include operator accountability details

## Operator Accountability

- The approving operator is responsible for the decision
- Uncertain approvals must be escalated or refused

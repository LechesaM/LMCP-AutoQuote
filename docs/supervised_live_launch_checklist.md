# Supervised-Live Launch Checklist

- Operator onboarding is completed before supervised-live launch.
- Confirm manual approval remains mandatory.
- Confirm `review_ready` remains mandatory.
- Confirm proof capture remains mandatory.
- Confirm final submission remains manual-only.
- Confirm operator authentication is enabled.
- Confirm operator roles are loaded.
- Confirm telemetry endpoints are read-only.
- Confirm dashboard fallback behavior is healthy.
- Confirm source health and queue health are visible.
- Confirm audit and activity timelines are append-only.

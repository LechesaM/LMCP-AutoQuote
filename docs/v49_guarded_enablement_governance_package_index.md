## Review Order
1. Read the change request to understand scope.
2. Review the policy for lock-clearing authority and audit requirements.
3. Complete the checklist against the evidence package.
4. Record the approval decision in the memo.

## Governance Notes
- Guarded V49 enablement remains subject to named operator execution.
- Lock-clear actions must be append-only governance events.
- Residual reversibility risk must be acknowledged explicitly.
- Broader rollout is out of scope until pilot evidence is reviewed.

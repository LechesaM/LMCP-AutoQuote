## Wave 1 Intake Checklist
### Cleaning Materials
- Capture at least one current supplier quote with unit price and delivery assumptions.
- Record pack sizes, hazard notes, and any exclusions.
- Prefer repeatable consumables with stable lead times.

### PPE
- Capture current pricing for core PPE SKUs used in tender screening.
- Record standards or sizing assumptions where relevant.
- Flag any items needing compliance evidence or special handling.

### Stationery
- Capture desk-use and bulk order pricing for common stationery lines.
- Record pack counts and brand alternates.
- Prioritize high-frequency replenishment items with stable pricing.

### Office Furniture
- Capture pricing for common furniture types, not bespoke one-offs.
- Record assembly, delivery, and lead-time assumptions.
- Prefer SKUs that recur across municipal and institutional buyers.

### Electrical Materials
- Capture current pricing for standard electrical consumables and fittings.
- Record amperage, voltage, or specification assumptions where relevant.
- Prefer repeatable items with clear technical descriptions.

### Plumbing Materials
- Capture pricing for common plumbing consumables and fittings.
- Record size, grade, and compatibility assumptions.
- Prioritize items that recur across maintenance and infrastructure tenders.

### Building Materials
- Capture pricing for core building materials with stable supply.
- Record unit conversions, delivery assumptions, and pack quantities.
- Prefer categories with reliable historical pricing evidence.

### Road Signs
- Capture pricing for standard sign types and mounting hardware.
- Record size, reflectivity, and installation assumptions.
- Prefer recurring municipal and transport-related sign variants.

### Traffic Accommodation
- Capture pricing for cones, barriers, and temporary control items.
- Record rental versus purchase assumptions if both are present.
- Prioritize items with predictable delivery and deployment terms.

### Water Treatment Consumables
- Capture pricing for common treatment consumables and chemicals.
- Record handling, storage, and compliance notes where relevant.
- Prefer items with clear product specs and stable demand.

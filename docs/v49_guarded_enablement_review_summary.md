## Decision Order
1. Approve the lock-clear authority matrix.
2. Approve the immutable lock-clear event procedure.
3. Approve the emergency override policy.
4. Approve the retention and audit policy.
5. Record residual risk acceptance or rejection.
6. Re-run the formal governance review and record the new outcome.

## Why Each Decision Matters
- Lock-clear authority matrix: defines who may clear submission locks and under what conditions.
- Immutable lock-clear event procedure: ensures each lock-clear action becomes an append-only governance event.
- Emergency override policy: defines whether emergency overrides are permitted and how they are supervised.
- Retention and audit policy: defines retention duration, audit cadence, archival, and recovery verification.
- Residual risk acceptance: records whether the remaining reversibility risk is formally accepted.
- Re-review: determines whether the platform remains in V48 or may proceed to a tightly supervised V49-A pilot.

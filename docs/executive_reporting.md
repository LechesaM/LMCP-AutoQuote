# Executive Reporting

The executive dashboard summarizes supervised-live performance for operators, supervisors and governance stakeholders.

## Executive KPIs

- RFQs harvested
- RFQs qualified
- RFQs reviewed
- Estimated profitability
- Operator throughput
- Queue pressure
- Governance incidents
- Source reliability
- SLA health

## Trend Interpretation

- Weekly and monthly trend windows show operational direction.
- Rolling averages smooth short-term noise.
- All trend outputs are advisory only.

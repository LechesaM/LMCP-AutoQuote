# Pricing Review Handbook

## Purpose
This handbook defines the operator review steps for pricing evidence and quote defensibility.

## Review Flow
1. Confirm the supplier record.
2. Confirm the product match.
3. Confirm the quoted unit and total values.
4. Confirm delivery assumptions.
5. Confirm VAT treatment.
6. Compare with historical pricing.
7. Apply the profitability rules library.
8. Record the confidence level and any override notes.

## Key Checks
- stale or expired quotes
- zero or negative values
- subtotal mismatches
- VAT mismatches
- missing freight assumptions
- unrealistic price deviation

## Output
Record:
- pricing outcome
- confidence level
- evidence source
- price age
- escalation notes

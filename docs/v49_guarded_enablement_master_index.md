## Review Sequence
1. Read the executive summary.
2. Review the policy documents.
3. Complete the checklist against the evidence set.
4. Record or update the decision log.
5. Use the remediation register to track remaining governance actions.

## Governance Reminder
- Guarded V49 enablement remains subject to explicit governance approval.
- Lock-clear actions must be append-only governance events.
- Residual reversibility must be formally acknowledged.
- Broad enablement remains out of scope until pilot review and governance approval.

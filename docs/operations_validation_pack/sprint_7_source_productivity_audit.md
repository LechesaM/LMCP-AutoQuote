# Sprint 7 Source Productivity Audit

## Purpose

## Method

- `runtime/live_rfqs.json`
- `runtime/rfq_lifecycle/rfqs.json`

## Dead-Weight Interpretation

In the observed pilot evidence, most of the 1,040 registry sources remain non-productive so far because they have not yet generated live-harvested RFQs.

That does not prove they are permanently dead. It means they are currently unproven under the existing pilot sample.

## Recommendation

- Keep the pilot frozen.
- Continue harvesting.
- Keep measuring source productivity before adding more sources.
- Prioritize source quality and harvest coverage before further registry growth.

## Operational Conclusion

The current limiting factor is not the size of the registry.

The limiting factor is that only a very small number of sources have produced live RFQs in the observed Sprint 7 evidence so far.

That makes source productivity the correct next focus area before any additional expansion is approved.

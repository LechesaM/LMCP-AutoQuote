# Supervised-Live Operator Feedback

## Feedback Basis
- Feedback is synthesized from the supervised-live evidence review and the current dashboard/reporting surfaces.
- This is operational feedback only and does not alter governance.

## Operator Workload
- Workload was manageable for the five-RFQ evidence set.
- Manual review burden increased on the technical fabrication and physical-submission cases.

## Workflow Clarity
- Workflow stages were clear.
- The manual approval, review_ready, and proof-capture checkpoints were easy to interpret.

## Dashboard Usefulness
- The dashboard surfaces are useful for tracking qualification, governance, and pricing evidence together.
- The advisory-only labels reduced ambiguity about what still requires human action.

## Quote-Pack Quality
- Quote-pack quality was strong for the eligible supply RFQs.
- Physical and technical RFQs still needed manual handling.

## Pricing Evidence Usefulness
- Pricing evidence reporting is useful when supplier quote artifacts are present.
- The evidence gap is visible when supplier quotes are missing, which is preferable to inventing a confidence score.

## RFQ Qualification Usefulness
- Qualification routing correctly separated the stronger supply case from the manual-review cases.
- Manual-review triggers were understandable for the technical fabrication and physical-submission RFQs.

## Friction Points
- Physical submission handling adds operator overhead.
- Technical validation requires more review time.
- Supplier evidence completeness is the biggest limiter on pricing confidence when live quote artifacts are absent.

## Manual-Review Pain Points
- Cases with submission complexity require more explicit operator notes.
- Missing supplier evidence reduces pricing defensibility and needs clearer capture discipline.

## Recommended Operational Improvements
- Keep a batch-level evidence checklist visible in the dashboard.
- Prompt operators to attach supplier quote artifacts before pricing confidence is finalized.
- Surface manual-review triggers earlier in the workflow.
- Continue to keep final submission manual-only.

# Engineering Evidence Levels

Use these levels when reporting readiness:

- **L0 — declared:** intent/default/static governance value only.
- **L1 — configured:** required configuration/artifact exists and is syntactically valid.
- **L2 — unit-validated:** isolated software behaviour passes automated unit tests.
- **L3 — integrated:** relevant components operate together in a controlled environment.
- **L4 — exercised:** operational procedure/failure mode has been deliberately exercised and observed.
- **L5 — measured:** capacity/performance/resilience/security claim is supported by reproducible measurements under the defined workload/environment.
- **L6 — certified/approved:** the applicable formal evidence bundle has passed required independent/human governance gates.

A higher-level production claim cannot be inferred from a lower evidence level without an explicit, documented reason and acceptance by the human authority.

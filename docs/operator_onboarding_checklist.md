# Operator Onboarding Checklist

- Supervised-live operators should understand the launch controls before taking actions.
- Confirm user account is provisioned.
- Confirm role assignment is correct.
- Confirm login succeeds.
- Confirm dashboard access is available.
- Confirm review queue access is available.
- Confirm operator actions are explicit and auditable.
- Confirm manual approval remains mandatory.
- Confirm `review_ready` remains mandatory.
- Confirm proof capture remains mandatory.
- Confirm manual approval and proof capture are understood.
- Confirm no autonomous submission or approval behavior exists.

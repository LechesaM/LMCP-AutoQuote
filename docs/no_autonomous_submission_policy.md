# No Autonomous Submission Policy

## Policy

Final submission is manual-only in LMCP AutoQuote.

## Prohibitions

- No autonomous final submission
- No bypass of approvals
- No bypass of review-ready state
- No silent proof capture
- No hidden submission side effects

## Operator Accountability

- Every final submission action must be attributable to a human operator
- Operators are responsible for the correctness of the submission decision

## Future Unlocking Requirements

Any future change to this policy would require explicit governance review, workflow redesign, and safety validation. This documentation set does not authorize such a change.

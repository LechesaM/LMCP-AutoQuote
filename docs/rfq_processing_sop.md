# RFQ Processing SOP

## How RFQs Enter the System

RFQs enter through governed intake, fixture-based validation, or operational ingestion paths that preserve manual-production control.

## Eligibility

An RFQ is eligible only when it fits the supply-and-delivery profile and satisfies the configured workflow and pricing rules.

## Exclusions

RFQs must be refused when they involve:

- medical consumables
- IT equipment
- petrol
- diesel
- catering
- compulsory briefing sessions that are required and not completed

## Pricing Floor

- Minimum profit: R30,000
- Minimum supply margin: 25%

RFQs below these thresholds must not progress to approval.

## Handling Uncertain RFQs

- Pause and inspect the source documents.
- Check category, buyer instructions, and compulsory briefing requirements.
- Check that the source RFQ is present and readable.
- Escalate if the RFQ cannot be classified confidently.

## Refusal Process

- Record the refusal reason clearly.
- Preserve the append-only workflow history.
- Do not advance the RFQ into approval or proof capture after refusal.

# Operator Productivity Optimization

LMCP productivity optimization improves review throughput, queue ergonomics, workload balancing, and governed bulk operations without changing workflow governance.

## Strategy
- Prioritize urgent and overdue reviews.
- Balance operator workload by active assignments, overdue items, queue lag, escalation load, and specialization.
- Surface evidence bottlenecks early so operators spend less time searching for missing context.
- Keep all actions explicit, reversible where practical, and auditable.

## Governance Protections
- No autonomous submission.
- No auto-approval.
- No `review_ready` bypass.
- No proof-capture bypass.
- Bulk actions require confirmation and supervisor or admin access.

## Throughput Optimization
- Queue ordering uses closing date, queue age, risk, pricing evidence, and governance blockers.
- Focus sessions and efficiency analytics identify where throughput is healthy or constrained.
- Queue heatmaps reveal concentration and load imbalances.

## Operator Ergonomics
- Keyboard shortcuts and queue presets reduce repetitive navigation.
- Bulk review actions are limited to governed administrative tasks.
- Review cards and panels emphasize the next safe manual action.

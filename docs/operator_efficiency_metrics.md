# Operator Efficiency Metrics

Tracked metrics include:
- RFQs reviewed per hour
- Review completion time
- Evidence handling time
- Escalation frequency
- Reassignment frequency
- Queue aging trends
- Operator throughput trends

## Operational Interpretation
- High review throughput is useful only when evidence quality and governance compliance remain intact.
- Queue age and stale evidence are stronger signals than raw volume alone.

## Anti-Abuse Guidance
- Do not use throughput metrics as disciplinary scoring.
- Do not optimize for speed at the expense of proof capture or review quality.

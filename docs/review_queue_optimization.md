# Review Queue Optimization

Queue optimization is advisory and governed.

## Prioritization Logic
- Near-closing RFQs move toward the top.
- Higher-value and high-confidence items are prioritized.
- Governance-blocked and stale RFQs are surfaced clearly.
- Evidence completeness and review readiness affect priority.

## Queue Balancing
- Operators should receive a balanced share of overdue and urgent work.
- Load recommendations are advisory only.
- No automatic reassignment is performed.

## Escalation Handling
- Urgent and high-priority items should be escalated manually when capacity is constrained.
- Escalations remain logged and reviewable.
